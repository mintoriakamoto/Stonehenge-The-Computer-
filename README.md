# Astronomy — What They Were Tracking

## Three Master Cycles Tracked Simultaneously

| Cycle | Period | Encoder | Precision | Purpose |
|-------|--------|---------|-----------|---------|
| **Solar Tropical Year** | 365.2422 days | Trilithon horseshoe axis + Heel Stone | ±0.5° azimuth = ±1 day | Agricultural calendar, midwinter renewal |
| **Lunar Nodal Cycle** | 18.618 years | Station Stone rectangle + Aubrey 56 counter | ±0.05 yr = ±18 days | Generational clock, eclipse seasons |
| **Lunar Standstill (Major)** | 18.618 yr extremes | Station Stone long sides | ±0.5° azimuth | Cosmic marriage (Sun⟂Moon) |

## The Solstitial Axis
- **Midwinter sunset**: 226.5° (SW) — primary alignment
- **Midsummer sunrise**: 46.5° (NE) — secondary
- Trilithon horseshoe opens NE, frames midwinter sunset
- Avenue (530m straight) on same axis
- Recent scholarship (Parker Pearson, English Heritage) argues **midwinter was primary**

## Station Stone Rectangle
- 4 stones (91, 92, 93, 94), only 2 survive
- **Short sides**: solstitial axis (SW-NE)
- **Long sides**: major lunar standstill (SE moonrise / NW moonset)
- **Only works at 51.18°N ±50km** — solstitial ⟂ standstill at this latitude
- Ruggles 1997: "deliberate alignment" — English Heritage 2024-25 live campaign

## The Aubrey 56 as Lunar Nodal Counter
- 56 holes = 3 × 18.618 = 55.854 (0.3% error)
- Move marker 3 holes/year → full cycle in 18.67 years
- **Drift**: 0.049 yr/step = 18 days per generation
- **Correction**: Leap-hole every 6 standstills (111.7 years)

## Heel Stone & Avenue
- Heel Stone (96): 80m from center, West Woods chemistry
- Stone 97: possible pair (Darvill) or source of 96 (Parker Pearson)
- Avenue: 2.5km to River Avon, first 530m straight on solstitial axis

## What Was NOT Tracked (Refuted)
- Planetary cycles (Venus, Mars, Jupiter) — no evidence
- Stellar proper motion (Orion/Sirius) — Burley 2016 unverified
- Eclipse prediction via Aubrey Holes — speculative (Hoyle), no archaeological support
- Precession of equinoxes — detected (Heel Stone drift) but not encoded
