## WHY PRESELI DOLERITE? (THE ACOUSTIC MATERIAL PHYSICS)

| Property | Preseli Dolerite | Local Chalk | Why It Mattered |
|----------|------------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Density | 2.9 g/cm³ | 1.8 g/cm³ | Mass for reflection |
| Elastic Modulus | 90 GPa | 30 GPa | Stiffness = sharp scatter |
| Internal Damping (Q⁻¹) | **~10⁻⁴** | ~10⁻² | **Rings 100× longer** |
| Crystal Structure | Interlocking plagioclase | Granular | Coherent scatter |
| Natural Shape | Columnar pillars | N/A | Quarry-ready geometry |

**Physics requirement**: The waveguide needed HIGH-FREQUENCY DIFFUSERS (250-500 Hz) that scatter energy vertically WITHOUT absorbing it. Only Preseli dolerite has the **low damping + high stiffness + natural pillar geometry** to do this.

**Cox 2020 proved it**: When bluestones removed → T₃₀ drops at 250/500 Hz. **They are the HF diffusers.**

## MATERIAL PHYSICS — WHY PRESELI DOLERITE RINGS

| Property | Value | Effect |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Sound velocity | 5,500-6,500 m/s | 2× chalk |
| Internal damping (Q⁻¹) | 10⁻⁴–10⁻³ | Low damping = high Q |
| Resonance Q | **1000–5000** | Rings for seconds |
| Residual stress | Pre-tuned lattice | "Pre-tuned" |

**Mechanism**: Residual compressive stress from cooling + tectonic compression → "pre-tuned" lattice. Magnetite/ilmenite exsolution → domain pinning → low damping. Cutting/embedding releases stress → **SILENCING**.

## ATOMIC vs PHONON vs MACROSCOPIC RESONANCE

| Domain | Frequency | Universal? | Stonehenge Value |
|--------|-----------|------------|------------------|
| Atomic electronic | 10¹⁵–10¹⁹ Hz | YES (NIST lines) | Fe I: 3581.2 Å |
| Lattice phonon | 10¹²–10¹³ Hz | YES (crystal) | θ_D ~ 600 K |
| **Macroscopic acoustic** | **10²–10⁴ Hz** | **NO (geometry)** | **~100–2000 Hz** |

**The "ringing" of bluestones is MACROSCOPIC — standing mechanical waves in a finite elastic body. Atomic composition enables it but does not equal it.**
